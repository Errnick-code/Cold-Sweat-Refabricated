**Cold Sweat Refabricated** is an unofficial port of [Cold Sweat](https://modrinth.com/mod/cold-sweat) by iMikul, chubbymomo and skinnymomo. All credit for the mod, its code, textures and balance goes to the original authors. The original source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Momo-Softworks/Cold-Sweat).

Cold Sweat is a Vanilla-friendly, deeply customizeable, and performant temperature mod for Minecraft. It leverages an advanced temperature system to give a well-rounded experience, packed with features that help or harm you.

A prepared player can weather the elements of Minecraft's hostile environments, but if you strand yourself in the middle of nowhere, there's no second chance!

## **~ Special Integration With ~**

**[Serene Seasons](https://modrinth.com/mod/serene-seasons)** — seasons change the temperature

**[Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-fly)** — pipes and tanks carry temperature, waterskins fill at the spouting station

**[Thirst Was Taken](https://modrinth.com/mod/thirst-was-taken-2)** — water purity and boiling, sea water stays salty

**[Trinkets Updated](https://modrinth.com/mod/trinkets)** — stands in for Curios, the thermometer goes into the charm slot

**[Biomes O' Plenty](https://modrinth.com/mod/biomes-o-plenty)**, **[Terralith](https://modrinth.com/mod/terralith)**, **[Oh, the Biomes You'll Go](https://modrinth.com/mod/oh-the-biomes-youll-go)** — biome temperature is taken from biome data

**[Curios](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/curios)**, **[Armor Underwear](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/armor-underwear-mod)**, **[Enhanced Visuals](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/enhancedvisuals)**, **[Vampirism](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/vampirism-become-a-vampire)** and **[Weather, Storms, & Tornadoes](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/weather-storms-tornadoes)** do not exist for Fabric, so those integrations are not in the port. The same goes for JEI and KubeJS.

<br>

*In vanilla, every biome has its own temperature. This mod uses that property for the temperature system, which means that this mod should be compatible with almost all modded biomes (given their temperatures are configured correctly ;) ).*
